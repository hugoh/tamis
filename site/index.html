a tamis is a drum sieve

Spam filtered. Mail sorted.

tamis watches your mailbox over IMAP: it files good mail into folders with Sieve rules, and re-checks what your provider's spam filter decided. It catches the spam that slipped through, rescues mail from people you know, and learns from every correction you make, in any mail client.

Get started View on GitHub

What it does

🚫 Catches missed spam

A Bayes filter trained on your mail scores everything that lands in the inbox and moves the spam to Junk.

🛟 Rescues good mail

Mail from verified senders you have written to comes straight out of Junk. The whitelist is built from your Sent folder and needs DKIM/DMARC to pass.

🎓 Learns from you

Drop mail in TrainSpam or TrainGood, move it to or from Junk, or press Junk / Not Junk. tamis picks it all up.

🗂️ Files the rest

Sieve rules sort good mail into folders, for providers like iCloud that have no server-side filtering to speak of.

🕶️ Starts in shadow mode

It only records what it would do until you trust it. Every decision is logged and can be undone.

🩺 Tells you when it breaks

A built-in health report and healthchecks.io pings alert you when tamis stops working.

How it works

  • Your provider files new mail
  • tamis re-checks it: whitelist, Bayes score, your corrections
  • Spam goes to Junk, good mail is rescued or filed by Sieve
  • You correct it anywhere; tamis learns

The web interface

Decisions, whitelist, rules, health and logs, protected by a passkey. This is the real page, rendered with sample data.

Quick start

tamis init --host imap.mail.me.com --user you@icloud.com
tamis login      # password goes to the system keychain
tamis bootstrap  # whitelist Sent recipients, train on Junk
tamis run        # starts in shadow mode
